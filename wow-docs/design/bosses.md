Source: Google Sheet "WoP Playtest, Documentation & Feedback", tabs *Rustboro Gym*, *Dewford Gym*, *Mauville Gym*, *Misc Bosses*. Species, move and item spellings are copied from the sheet as written (some contain typos, e.g. "Mangeton", "omnious wind", "Gryo Ball"). The in-game data is the source of truth for exact values.

For the original-trainer to boss mapping and what has been tested, see [../tracking/trainer-mapping.md](../tracking/trainer-mapping.md). Design inspiration notes per boss are in [../playtest/feedback.md](../playtest/feedback.md).

**Victory scene (v0.1.3):** the defeat text narrates Sulfuras falling and Flannery barely breathing. The player is warped to the Lavaridge Pokemon Center, Nurse Joy takes Flannery, the screen fades, and Magni (who appears only during the scene, `FLAG_GJT_HIDE_LAVARIDGE_MAGNI`, `VAR_GJT_LAVARIDGE_SCENE`) hands out the Heat Badge, Overheat TM, and flags. Flannery is hidden in the gym afterwards (`FLAG_GJT_HIDE_LAVARIDGE_FLANNERY`), so her rematch is unreachable for now.

## Mossdeep Gym — Windrunner sisters (Ghost/Dark, cap 42) — IN PROGRESS

Leader fight: Sylvanas + Alleria as one double battle (properly hard). Decidueye is reserved for Sylvanas.
Lieutenants (replace all 12 vanilla trainers, 2 per warp cluster): Nathanos + Varimathras (Sylvanas's side), Arator + Turalyon (Alleria's family), Rhonin + Vereesa (Vereesa is the final lieutenant before the sisters).

### Nathanos Blightcaller (`TRAINER_PRESTON`, Pkmn Ranger) — drafted
Gimmick: Hunter's Mark. Holds 4 Dire Hit; AI uses one on each mon's first turn (about 50% crit chance, no other crit boosts stacked). Opponent-side use prints "HUNTER'S MARK! <mon> is lining up a killing shot!" (`STRINGID_GJT_HUNTERS_MARK`, `BattleScript_ItemSetFocusEnergy`).
Team (IVs 20): Houndoom 37 (Crunch, Fire Fang, Sucker Punch, Dark Pulse), Weavile 38 (Night Slash, Ice Shard, Knock Off, Brick Break), Fearow 39 (Sniper; Drill Peck, Aerial Ace, Return, U-turn), Dartrix 40 ace (Eviolite; Leaf Blade, Brave Bird, Shadow Sneak, Swords Dance).

### Varimathras (`TRAINER_VIRGIL`, name "VARIMATH", Psychic placeholder sprite) — drafted
Gimmick: debuffs. Incineroar leads (Intimidate + U-turn pivots; Smart Switching so the AI can bring it back), Will-O-Wisp/Toxic support, Zoroark second (Illusion copies the LAST party mon, so it must not be last; it poses as Skuntank). No Ace flag, so order is fixed. Holds a Full Restore.
Team (IVs 20, in order): Incineroar 40 (Sitrus; Flare Blitz, Darkest Lariat, Knock Off, U-turn), Zoroark 41 (Illusion; Double Team, Night Daze, Flamethrower, Sludge Bomb), Crobat 38 (Cross Poison, Air Slash, Will-O-Wisp, Toxic), Skuntank 39 (Aftermath; Poison Jab, Crunch, Will-O-Wisp, Sucker Punch).

### Arator the Hopeful (`TRAINER_BLAKE`, name "ARATOR", Expert placeholder sprite) — drafted
Child of void and light. Joke: he is immature, so his first three mons are badly underleveled (33-35, IVs 12) but all carry Counter/Mirror Coat so careless hits get punished. The ace is far stronger: Lucario 42 (cap-level), IVs 31, 252 Atk / 252 Spe, Focus Sash, Justified (Dark moves in this gym power it up), Revenge.
Team: Togekiss 33 (Serene Grace; Air Slash, Mirror Coat, Nasty Plot, Roost), Gardevoir 34 (Trace; Moonblast, Counter, Calm Mind, Shadow Ball), Spiritomb 35 (Infiltrator; Foul Play, Counter, Will-O-Wisp, Shadow Sneak), Lucario 42 ace (Close Combat, Extreme Speed, Revenge, Swords Dance).
TODO when the gym layout is done: intro/defeat text should play up the immaturity.

### Turalyon (`TRAINER_NATE`, name "TURALYON") — drafted
Lightforged Paladin, the holy tank who holds the line. Steel resists Ghost and Dark, so he walls the gym theme. Stealth Rock + Wish stalling. Pairs with Arator in the same cluster.
Team (IVs 24): Bastiodon 38 (Leftovers; Sturdy; Stealth Rock, Iron Head, Rock Slide, Roar), Blissey 39 (Natural Cure; Wish, Seismic Toss, Toxic, Heal Bell), Aegislash 40 (Stance Change; King's Shield, Sacred Sword, Shadow Ball, Iron Head), Aggron 41 ace (Sitrus; Sturdy; Heavy Slam, Earthquake, Rock Slide, Thunder Wave).

### Rhonin (`TRAINER_NICHOLAS`, name "RHONIN", Kindler sprite) — drafted
Vereesa's husband, the red-haired fire mage. Mid-difficulty so Vereesa is the real test. Ace is the custom Fire Mage class mon (Fire/Dragon, Hot Streak, Pyroblast + Phoenix Flames). Party species name is `Mage-Fire` in trainers.party.
Team (IVs 22): Alakazam 38 (Magic Guard; Psychic, Shadow Ball, Focus Blast, Calm Mind), Magmortar 38 (Flame Body; Flamethrower, Thunderbolt, Focus Blast, Psychic), Chandelure 39 (Spell Tag; Flash Fire; Shadow Ball, Flamethrower, Energy Ball, Will-O-Wisp), Mage-Fire 40 ace (Charcoal; Hot Streak; Pyroblast, Phoenix Flames, Flamethrower, Blast Wave).

### Vereesa (`TRAINER_KATHLEEN`, name "VEREESA", Pkmn Ranger F) — drafted
Ranger General; final and toughest lieutenant (5 mons, IVs 26). Normal/Steel core with a Psychic and a Fairy. Normal is immune to Ghost and Steel resists Ghost/Dark, so she walls the gym theme. Volley flavor via Skill Link Cinccino.
Team: Staraptor 39 (Intimidate; Brave Bird, Close Combat, Double-Edge, U-turn), Cinccino 39 (Skill Link; Bullet Seed, Tail Slap, Rock Blast, U-turn), Mawile 40 (Intimidate; Iron Head, Play Rough, Sucker Punch, Swords Dance), Espeon 40 (Magic Bounce; Psychic, Shadow Ball, Calm Mind, Dazzling Gleam), Metagross 41 ace (Sitrus; Clear Body; Meteor Mash, Zen Headbutt, Earthquake, Bullet Punch).
