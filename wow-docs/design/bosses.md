# Boss teams

Source: Google Sheet "WoP Playtest, Documentation & Feedback", tabs *Rustboro Gym*, *Dewford Gym*, *Mauville Gym*, *Misc Bosses*. Species, move and item spellings are copied from the sheet as written (some contain typos, e.g. "Mangeton", "omnious wind", "Gryo Ball"). The in-game data is the source of truth for exact values.

Format: Pokémon, level, ability, nature, moves, item. "ace" marks the team's ace.

For the original-trainer to boss mapping and what has been tested, see [../tracking/trainer-mapping.md](../tracking/trainer-mapping.md). Design inspiration notes per boss are in [../playtest/feedback.md](../playtest/feedback.md).
