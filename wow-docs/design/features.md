# Feature overview

Source: Google Sheet "WoP Playtest, Documentation & Feedback", tab *Overview* (May 2025, playtest build).

## Quality of life

- EXP. All from the beginning, with a slight global XP boost (must be used from the bag)
- Hard level caps (see [level-caps.md](level-caps.md))
- All Pokémon through Gen 9, including learnsets
- Old Rod available on Route 103
- Increased starting money (subject to change)
- EVs are only given and removed through items, to give a "skill point" RPG feel
- EVs and IVs are visible, somewhat, on the summary screen, graded on a roughly normal distribution (F and S are outstanding)
- Free relearns from the summary screen
- Reusable TMs

## Content updates (as of the playtest build)

- Encounter tables (specified in images emailed to playtesters for nuzlocke routing; the images are not in this repo)
- Plot
- Custom mons and evolutions: 8 at the time (sheet: "1+ 3 specs for two classes")
- Custom moves: about 20
- Custom abilities: 2
- Updated boss fights. The game is intended to be fairly easy outside of gyms, team encounters and other major boss battles.
