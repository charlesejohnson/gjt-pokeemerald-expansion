# Mossdeep gym texts

Story frame: the Windrunners and their lieutenants are a group separate from Khadgar and the player. They hold mixed views (conquer this new world vs rescue Azeroth), are bound by blood and opportunity in a strange land, and are not exactly allies. Tone: snarky, humorous, sore losers. Lines are in `data/maps/MossdeepCity_Gym/scripts.inc` (the lieutenant keeps its old vanilla label, e.g. `Preston` = Nathanos).

## Nathanos (snarky loyalist / sore loser)
- Intro: "The Dark Lady wants to know who comes through her door. I'll mark you. That way I never have to ask twice."
- Defeat: "Missed. I never miss. I'll tell Lady SYLVANAS it was the lighting."
- After: "Go on, then. Don't tell her I lost. I'll say it was a draw."

## Varimathras (classic-era: polite, servile, treacherous; "my lady", always on the winning side)
- Intro: "A visitor. Do forgive the dark. My lady insists. I have always served the winning side. It is simply not yours."
- Defeat: "Impressive. Truly. I shall remember this. I am very good at remembering."
- After: "Do give her my regards. I'd say my loyalty, but I think we both know better."

## Arator (immature, over-the-top righteous, whiny; a smidge of parents, mostly the Light)
- Intro: "HALT, in the name of the LIGHT! The LIGHT sees all! And it says you're going down! By the power of friendship!"
- Defeat: "That's NOT fair! The LIGHT was still warming up! I was going easy! ...Okay, I wasn't. Please don't tell Mom."
- After: "Ugh... the real treasure... was the cozy friends we made along the way..."
