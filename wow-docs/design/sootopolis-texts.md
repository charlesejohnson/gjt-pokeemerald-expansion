# Sootopolis gym texts

## Mekkatorque (cheerful, fast-talking gnome inventor)
- Intro: "Ah, a visitor! Excellent timing! My latest prototype is fully tested! Ninety percent tested! Okay, forty."
- Ace line (trainer slide, last switch-in): "We've come too far to let this responsibility fall to someone else!"
- Defeat: "Fascinating! The explosion was supposed to be smaller. I'll note that."
- After: "Taelia's ahead. She's wonderful. Her armor does not have a safety feature either."

## Taelia Fordragon (earnest, warm, won't go easy)
- Intro: "Jaina is just ahead. I'm to make sure you're ready for her. I'm very fond of you already. I'm still not going to go easy."
- Defeat: "Oh, well fought! Truly! I mean it. I'm a little bit proud of you."
- After: "Jaina has a lot on her mind. Her father most of all. Be kind, and be careful." (hints at the Daelin secret)

## Daelin Proudmoore (secret boss; gruff, bitter, formal, hostile to the intruder)
- Intro: "You fell. Into MY hold. Most would have stayed down. Let me teach you what the sea does to the stubborn."
- Ace line (slide, Kingdra out): "I've lost a fleet. I've lost a daughter's trust. I will not lose to a child!"
- Defeat: "Hmph. The sea favors you today. Do not mistake that for respect."
- Reward (5 of each vitamin, one-time): "My supplies. Salted, stale, and mine. Take them. Perhaps they will do you more good than they did my fleet."
- After: "Take it, and go. A man pays what he owes. Even a bad one."

## Jaina (in Juan's body; cool, tired, determined to be the one who rescues Azeroth)
- Intro: "You're the one KHADGAR sent. Good. I haven't the patience to be cruel today. You know, at first I was upset about being put in this body. But his sense of fashion and hair are fantastic. It will do for now. I'll be honest with you. Everyone here wants AZEROTH back. I want to be the one who brings it back. I have buried too much of it already to let someone else try. So I can't lose to you. Fight well, and I will do the same."
